About

Hello!

My research sits at the intersection of psychiatric genetics (“Discovery”) and the responsible communication of findings to diverse audiences (“Dialogue”).

Discovery: I specialise in psychiatric genetics, with a particular interest in eating disorders and their genetic overlap with other health-related conditions.

Dialogue: I am also interested in how psychiatric genomic discoveries are responsibly communicated, accurately understood, and ultimately translated into meaningful public engagement.

Research

What I study

01

Eating disorder genetics

Investigating the genetic architecture of anorexia nervosa and binge eating.

02

Eating disorder epidemiology, risk factors, and comorbidities

Exploring the complexities of eating disorders, including their causes and multi-system impact on the human body and function.

03

Psychiatric genomics & society

Studying how genetic explanations in psychiatry are responsibly and accurately communicated.

Projects

Current work

01 · Genetic epidemiology

Genetics, puberty & binge eating

Examining how genetic liability, pubertal development and reproductive hormones contribute to the development of binge eating, using genetic epidemiology and longitudinal approaches.

02 · Psychiatric genomics

Communicating psychiatric genetics

Exploring how biogenetic explanations of mental health are communicated and understood, with the aim of supporting responsible, accessible and non-stigmatising communication.

03 · Population data

Eating disorders across the life course

Using large-scale cohort and genetic datasets to investigate developmental timing, metabolic factors and biological pathways associated with eating disorder traits.
