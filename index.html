About

Hello!

My research sits at the intersection of psychiatric genetics (“Discovery”) and the responsible communication of findings to diverse audiences (“Dialogue”).

Discovery: I specialise in psychiatric genetics, with a particular interest in eating disorders and their genetic overlap with other health-related conditions.

Dialogue: I am also interested in how psychiatric genomic discoveries are responsibly communicated, accurately understood, and ultimately translated into meaningful public engagement.
